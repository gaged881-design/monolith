How it works

  1. Orders arrive by emailInbox reads your mailboxes (read-only) and turns retailer confirmations, shipping and cancellation emails into orders and shipments.
  2. Scan it inReceive against the tracking number with a USB scanner, your webcam or your phone. Shortages and damage are recorded.
  3. Sell and reconcileScan items out as sold. The sale is matched to that unit's own cost, so profit per sale is real.
MONOLITH INBOX

Inventory, orders and receiving on your own PC.

No MONOLITH cloud account exists or is needed. The app runs its own small server on your computer; your phone connects over your Wi-Fi, or privately from anywhere with Tailscale.

  • Orders from email, automatically. Up to 10 mailboxes over IMAP, read-only, including the spam folder, because forwarded retailer mail often lands there. Inventory CSV import too.
  • Canceled stays canceled. An order that was ever canceled never counts again, and $0 lines never count.
  • Real cost basis. Permanent SKUs, UPCs and custom barcodes; purchase lots with unit cost and age; an append-only ledger so every unit is explainable.
  • Locations. Warehouse, bins, store, show, vending machines. Archive products without losing history.
  • Scan everything. Receive, move, allocate, sell, count, restock, damage, return, adjust. An unknown barcode can be linked to a product on the spot.
  • Receiving against tracking numbers with shortages, damage and over-receipts recorded.
  • Live feed of order activity with spend per item and a shareable summary image.
  • Market values next to cost, from a public daily TCG price mirror.
  • iPhone companion: dashboard, camera scanning, inventory with costs, receiving.
  • Team members with roles (admin, manager, staff, viewer). Webhooks and notification rules.
  • Encrypted one-file backup and restore, and guided setup of a second PC as an extra screen.

MONOLITH Toolbox

One key, entered once.

Toolbox is the only thing you download. It asks for your key, then installs, updates and opens both apps. The apps always open through Toolbox; desktop shortcuts and start-with-Windows do that for you automatically.

  1. Pick a planYour license key arrives by email, usually within a few minutes.
  2. Download ToolboxAbout 3 MB. No account, no sign-in.
  3. Paste your keyIt is checked on your PC. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  4. Install, then OpenOne click each for Inbox and Radar. Updates are one click too.

Every download is verified

Before an installer is allowed to run, Toolbox checks its SHA-256 checksum and its cryptographic signature against MONOLITH's signing key. A tampered or corrupted file is refused. The apps' own "Check for updates" buttons do the same.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Can I buy just one app?

No. MONOLITH INBOX and MONOLITH TCG RADAR are sold together as one bundle.

Do I need a key for each app?

No. One key, entered once in Toolbox, opens both.

Does the Lifetime key ever expire?

No. A Lifetime key has no expiry date and never needs renewing. Keep the purchase email: the key is how you reinstall or move to a new PC.

Where is my data?

On your own computer. Mailbox passwords are kept in Windows Credential Manager on your PC and are never sent to us. The license key is checked offline; the apps do not phone home.

Do I lose my data if I cancel?

No. Nothing is deleted. The apps stop opening until a valid key is entered, and then everything is as you left it.

My key has not arrived.

It is usually there within a few minutes. If it has not arrived in 30 minutes, check spam, then contact support with your Whop receipt.

Toolbox says my key has expired.

Look for the most recent key email (a fresh key is sent at every renewal) and paste it with "Change key". If your plan ended, pick a plan again. Your data is safe either way.

Is this a checkout bot?

No. Neither app adds to cart, purchases, solves CAPTCHAs or uses proxies.
