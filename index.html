MONOLITH Toolbox

One key, entered once.

Toolbox is the only thing you download. It asks for your key, then installs, updates and opens Radar. Radar always opens through Toolbox; your desktop shortcut and start-with-Windows do that for you.

  1. Pick a planYour license key arrives by email, usually within a few minutes.
  2. Download ToolboxAbout 3 MB. No account, no sign-in.
  3. Paste your keyIt is checked on your PC and used to read the MONOLITH feed.
  4. Install, then OpenOne click each. Updates are one click too.

Every download is verified

Before an installer is allowed to run, Toolbox checks its SHA-256 checksum and its cryptographic signature against MONOLITH's signing key. A tampered or corrupted file is refused. Radar's own "Check for updates" does the same.

Getting started

From key to first alert in a few minutes.

Set up

  • Install Toolbox, paste your key, click Install on MONOLITH TCG RADAR, then Open.
  • Alerts start appearing on their own. Nothing to connect.
  • Press Watch on anything you want a notification for, or add words under Watching.
  • Settings → Notifications → "Send a test" shows you what a notification looks like.

Renewed, or a new PC?

Paste the new key with "Change key" in Toolbox. On a new PC, install Toolbox and paste the same key.

Something not working?

Radar shows "Not connected" with the reason in the bottom-left corner. A key that has run out says so; check your latest key email or your plan on Whop.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What does Radar actually do?

It shows you the alerts posted to the MONOLITH channels, as they happen, and sends a Windows notification for the products or words you picked. It does not buy anything, add anything to a cart, or touch any retailer's website itself.

Do I need Discord?

No. Radar reads the MONOLITH feed on its own. You never add a bot, a webhook or a channel.

Does the Lifetime key ever expire?

No. A Lifetime key has no expiry date and never needs renewing. Keep the purchase email: the key is how you reinstall or move to a new PC.

What does Radar send from my PC?

Your license key, to the MONOLITH feed, to prove your plan is active. Nothing else: no browsing, no account, no analytics. Your pick-list stays on your computer.

Do I lose anything if I cancel?

No. Radar stops reading the feed until a valid key is entered; your pick-list is still there.

My key has not arrived.

It is usually there within a few minutes. If it has not arrived in 30 minutes, check spam, then contact support with your Whop receipt.

Toolbox says my key has expired.

Look for the most recent key email (a fresh key is sent at every renewal) and paste it with "Change key". If your plan ended, pick a plan again.

When is INBOX coming?

It is being finished now. When it ships it will appear in Toolbox as a second Install button, at no extra cost, for every active plan.
