Privacy

Plain version: the apps keep your data on your computer, and we do not run a cloud service that receives it.

The apps

  • MONOLITH TCG RADAR keeps its settings and your pick-list on your computer. There is no MONOLITH account.
  • Radar reads the MONOLITH alert feed, which runs on our own computer. To prove your plan is active it sends your license key with each request; the feed answers with the alerts and nothing else is collected about you. What you pick is never sent.
  • Radar does not visit any retailer's website and does not connect to Discord. Alerts are collected on our side and relayed to you.
  • Radar also contacts the public release folder on GitHub to check for and download signed updates.
  • MONOLITH Toolbox verifies your key on your PC and downloads installers from that same public release folder.

Buying

Checkout, billing and subscriptions are handled by Whop. Whop collects what it needs to take payment and tells us about the sale (your name, email address, the plan and the payment id) so a license key can be generated and emailed to you. We keep a ledger of issued keys: licensee, email, plan dates and the payment id.

This website

The website is static. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics or third-party scripts. It remembers one preference (the colour of the background animation) in your browser's local storage. The host (GitHub Pages) keeps ordinary server logs under its own policy.
